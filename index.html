Tuck for speed, slide to scrub it before the bends. Stay in your lane, watch for cars and pass close for bonus points.

Above about 72 km/h the board can get speed wobbles: tuck, stay smooth or throw a slide. Steer out of a slide to close it before you let go, or the wheels hook up and throw you.
