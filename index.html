SETTINGS

Turn the screen like a steering wheel. However you're holding it when you press RIDE (or carry on after a pause) is straight ahead. Swipe up and down, tap and flick work as normal.

Changing the graphics restarts the game.

YOUR STARS

Keep them safe: save a backup to Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Load it to get them back.

Played in Safari and the home screen app? They keep their stars apart. Press COPY where your stars are, then PASTE in the other one.

Tuck for speed, slide to scrub it before the bends. Stay in your lane, watch for cars and pass close for bonus points.

Above about 45 mph (72 km/h) the board can get speed wobbles: tuck, stay smooth or throw a slide. Steer out of a slide to close it before you let go, or the wheels hook up and throw you.
